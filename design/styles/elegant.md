# Elegant — "Ledger & Letterhead"

**Who it's for:** consultants, architects, agencies, and studios billing for high-value work,
where the invoice itself is part of the professional impression.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink navy | `#1B2430` | Primary text, header band |
| Ivory | `#F7F4EC` | Page background |
| Brass | `#B08D57` | Accent — hairline rules only, never a fill |
| Slate | `#5B6472` | Secondary text (labels, meta) |
| Paper white | `#FFFFFF` | Card/document surface |

Almost monochrome. Brass appears only as thin rule lines — never as a background wash or button fill.

## Type

- **Headings:** Cormorant Garamond (classic display serif, old-style figures for amounts)
- **Body / data:** Inter (neutral, precise — keeps the ledger legible)

## Layout

- No card borders, no shadows, no border-radius — flat ink-on-paper, like a printed letterhead
- Single thin brass hairline under the header block; a double hairline above the grand total
  (a classic ledger convention, not decoration)
- Generous outer margins, as if laid out for a physical letter
- Items table: hairline row dividers only, right-aligned numeric columns
- Grand total is set larger in the serif, not boxed or colored — weight does the work

## Principle

*Restraint signals trust.* Nothing is bolded for attention or colored for emphasis — the
hierarchy comes entirely from type scale, spacing, and the discipline of the hairline rules.
